MoneyRabbit has been deployed!

To access the frontend, port-forward the service:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "moneyrabbit.frontend.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.frontend.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Then open http://localhost:3000

Backend API:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "moneyrabbit.backend.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.backend.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Swagger UI: http://localhost:8080/docs/swagger/index.html
